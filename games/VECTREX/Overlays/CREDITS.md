# Vectrex overlay credits

Standard artwork is at the archive root; clockwise and counterclockwise
versions are in Tate. Mine_Storm_(strategy) is normal orientation only.
Each ART contains 1080p, 720p, 480p and 240p artwork; 480i uses the 480p plane.

Conversion includes resizing, alpha preparation and ART encoding. Restoration
means rebuilding or cleaning up source artwork. Original creators retain credit.
